2. What information we collect

  • When you use our contact form: your name, email address, the topic you choose, and the message you write. Please don't include sensitive details you wouldn't want us to hold.
  • When you contact us directly by email or phone: whatever you choose to tell us, such as your contact details and information about a property.
  • Technical information: like most websites, the services that deliver this site may record basic technical data such as your IP address and browser type. We do not use this to identify you.

3. How we use it, and why

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss properties, development opportunities or investment.
  • To keep a record of our conversations.
  • To publish a testimonial, if you gave us one, with the name and description you agreed to.

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests (running our business and replying to people who contact us) and, where you ask us to take steps before entering into an agreement, taking those steps at your request. We do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to, and we never sell your information.

4. Who we share it with

We only share information with services that help us run the website and our business:

  • Formspree processes our contact form and passes your message to our email. It also keeps a copy of submissions so that we can access them.
  • Our email provider receives and stores the messages we get.
  • GitHub hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts used on this site. Your browser requests them from Google when a page loads, which means your IP address is sent to Google.

We may also share information where the law requires it. Some of these providers are based outside the UK, so your information may be processed in other countries, including the United States. Where that happens we rely on appropriate legal safeguards.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 12 months after our last contact with you, unless we need to keep them longer to carry out an agreement or meet a legal obligation. We keep a published testimonial until you ask us to remove it.

6. Cookies

This website does not set cookies of its own and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, we will update this page.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to our use of it;
  • ask for your information in a portable format, where this applies.

To use any of these rights, email us at sam.best@sambestabodeslimited.com. We will reply within one month.

8. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
