About us

Sam Best Abodes Ltd is a property investment and development company focused on identifying and transforming underutilised buildings into high-quality residential accommodation across Bridgwater and the surrounding towns.

Our primary focus is acquiring properties with strong development potential, particularly buildings that can be converted into premium co-living apartments. These meet the growing demand for modern, affordable and community-focused living, and we are aware of the local need for housing for Hinkley Point contractors.

We are committed to creating attractive, professionally managed homes that enhance local communities while delivering sustainable long-term value. At the heart of everything we do is a commitment to quality, integrity and lasting relationships with property owners, investors and local partners.

Interiors with a story

What sets us apart is our passion for design. We love transforming ordinary spaces into homes with character, with interiors that are quirky, fun and different.

We use recycled furniture and reused homeware to create lovely homes, though all bedding and towels are freshly new. We like interiors that enhance the building and tell its story.

Our mission

Our goal is not simply to invest in property but to breathe new life into buildings, enhance local communities, and create spaces that people are proud to call home.

Investors

We provide opportunities to invest in carefully selected property projects with the goal of delivering attractive returns through a combination of rental income and capital growth. By focusing on high-potential properties in Bridgwater and surrounding areas, we aim to create long-term value while transforming buildings into exceptional homes and commercial spaces.

While property values can rise and fall, our investment approach is centred on protecting capital and maximising potential returns through strategic property development.
